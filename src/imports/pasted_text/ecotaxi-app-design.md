Design a modern premium mobile app UI/UX for an eco-friendly taxi service called “EcoTaxi”.

The product is a modern ride-hailing platform inspired by Uber and Yandex Go, but with a strong environmental identity. The interface must feel premium, clean, trustworthy, fast and technologically advanced.

TARGET AUDIENCE:
Young adults, professionals and environmentally conscious users aged 18–45.

DESIGN STYLE:
- Premium modern fintech/transportation aesthetic
- Minimalistic and clean
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Rounded cards and buttons
- Large interactive map areas
- Soft shadows
- Smooth spacing
- Modern typography
- High-quality UI suitable for a real commercial startup
- Avoid childish or overly “green” environmental visuals
- Eco identity should feel technological rather than natural/organic

COLOR SYSTEM:
Primary: deep emerald green
Secondary: fresh green
Background: very light neutral / white
Text: dark charcoal
Accent: electric lime used sparingly
Error: red
Success: green

Use green as the main brand color but keep the interface mostly clean and neutral.
